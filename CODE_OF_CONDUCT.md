# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming. Many contributors here are students building their first open-source project.
- Be respectful of different opinions, experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, never about the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/DhairyaSanathara). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments,
commits or issues, or block contributors who break this code of conduct, following the enforcement guidelines of
the Contributor Covenant.
